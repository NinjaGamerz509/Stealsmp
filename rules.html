Know Before You Play

Server Rules

Breaking rules results in punishment. Ignorance is not an excuse — read carefully.

info

Rules are enforced by our staff team. Punishments scale with severity and repeat offences. If you believe a punishment was unfair, open a ticket in our Discord server. Staff have final say on all decisions.

menu_book
General Rules
1
Respect All Players
Treat everyone with basic respect. Harassment, hate speech, racism, sexism, or any form of targeted toxicity will not be tolerated under any circumstances.
Ban
2
No Cheating or Hacking
Use of hacked clients, X-ray, fly hacks, kill-aura, auto-clickers, or any software that gives an unfair advantage is strictly forbidden.
Perm Ban
3
No Bug Exploiting
Exploiting server bugs or glitches for personal gain is prohibited. Report bugs to staff immediately via Discord — do not abuse them.
Ban
4
No Impersonating Staff
Pretending to be a staff member or admin to mislead other players is a serious offence and will result in an immediate ban.
Perm Ban
5
English / Respectful Language in Global Chat
Keep global chat in English so everyone can participate. Excessive spamming, caps lock abuse, or flooding chat is not allowed.
Mute
sports_kabaddi
PvP Rules
6
No Combat Logging
Logging out during active combat to avoid dying is considered combat logging and is a punishable offence. Stay and fight — or accept the loss.
Temp Ban
7
No Spawn Camping
Camping spawn or safe zones to repeatedly kill new or respawning players is not allowed. PvP must happen in the open world.
Warn
8
No Teaming in Solo Events
Teaming up in solo PvP events or arenas is forbidden. Compete fairly within the intended format of each game mode.
Warn
chat
Chat Rules
9
No Advertising
Promoting other Minecraft servers, Discord links, YouTube channels, or external sites in chat is not allowed.
Perm Mute
10
No Threats or Doxing
Threatening players in real life, sharing personal information, or attempting to dox anyone will result in an immediate permanent ban with no appeal.
Perm Ban
11
No Scamming
Scamming other players in trades, agreements, or real-money transactions is prohibited. All deals should be honoured. Staff will investigate scam reports.
Ban
12
Follow Staff Instructions
If a staff member asks you to stop doing something, comply immediately. Ignoring or disrespecting staff decisions will escalate your punishment.
Temp Ban
